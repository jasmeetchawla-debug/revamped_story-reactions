What each control does

  • Tap left / rightPreviousStory / NextStory. Past the last story it jumps to the next user, then closes.
  • HoldPause while held, Resume on release. Progress bar freezes.
  • Activity chip (owner)One 32 px chip, bottom-left: two viewer avatars + "48 views" | heart + count. Each half opens its tab. Delete moved to the header, so the bottom row is gone. Shimmers until confirmed.
  • Heart burstWhen a story has reactions, hearts float up from the heart half once on load and it pulses. 0 reactions: heart stays as an outline, no burst. Skipped under reduced motion.
  • Shrinking storyOpening the sheet scales only the story media into the space under the header (400 ms, same easing as the snips comments sheet). Progress bars and the header stay full size. Close with X or Esc.
  • Activity sheetHalf-height, compact rows. One sheet, two tabs: All (viewers, reactors get a heart badge) and Reactions. Opens on Reactions from the heart half, All from the views half. Story pauses.
  • Edge cases0 reactions: heart half stays as an outline with 0, Reactions tab shows an empty message. 0 views: chip reads "0 views" with the same outline heart; the sheet shows "No views yet".
  • DataSheet merges the existing viewers and reactions endpoints by userId on the client. No backend change.
  • Reactions (viewer)A separate transparent chip above the field: reactor avatars + "6 reactions" (outline heart + "0 reactions" when empty). Opens the Reactions sheet. Like and share stay as their own 44 px icons. Viewer counts stay owner-only.
  • Reply field (viewer)Its own outlined field. Focus pauses the story and darkens it slightly. While typing, like and share swap for a Send button; Enter also sends (SendStoryReply).
  • Share sheetMirrors the real ShareBottomSheet: search, multi-select connections, Copy link, Share via, Add to Story (hidden when canShareToStory is false, see story 3), message field and Send with a count.
  • Trash (owner, header)Confirmation dialog, then DeleteStory. Cancel resumes.
